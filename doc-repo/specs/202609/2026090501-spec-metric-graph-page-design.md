The main purposes of this page is to give users a comprehensive, dynamic view of the metrics, their ontology, to help users understand metrics and how they are processed in the system.

This page has three major components:
1. A graph drawn on an (appear to be) unlimited canvas
2. A tabbed content viewer
3. A PDF document viewer


The three components can be arranged in one of the following setup:
1. Left-Right: the Left panel is divided in two panels: Left-Up and Left-Bottom. The Left-Up panel shows the canvas. The Left-Button shows the tabbed content viewer and the Right panel shows the PDF display viewer.
2. Top-Bottom: The Top panel shows the canvas. The Bottom is divided in Bottom-Left and Bottom-Right. The Bottom-Left shows the tabbed content viewer and the Bottom-Right shows the PDF display viewer.

The width/height of the panels should be slide-adjustable.

The page is added to `ChenWeb/home3/knowledge, Ontology => Metric Ontology Explorer`.
